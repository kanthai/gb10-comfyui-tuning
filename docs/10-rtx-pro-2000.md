# 10. RTX PRO 2000 Blackwell (sm_120, 16 GB) in a VM

A single-slot RTX PRO 2000 Blackwell passed through to a VM, power-capped at **60 W** (single-slot
cooler thermals), runs Krea 2 image generation and RTX Video Super Resolution upscaling.
ComfyUI 0.37, Python 3.13, comfy-kitchen 0.2.35.

## Ceilings

mma.sync peaks at 2527 MHz: NVFP4 342 / INT8 176 / FP8 165 / BF16 92 TOPS. But it is
**power-bound** in practice: sustained BF16 GEMM 31.2 TFLOPS at 60 W (SM clock ~1100 MHz,
50→70 °C in 40 s) vs 36.1 TFLOPS at 70 W (+16%, 76 °C, fan 91%). The 60 W cap stays.

This is also why the GB10 is ~2x faster on the same Krea workload: not VRAM streaming, the power
cap.

## What helped

1. `--fast fp8_matrix_mult` on ComfyUI (systemd drop-in on the service's `ExecStart`): raw FP8
   checkpoints then use FP8 tensor cores instead of upcasting to BF16 each step (activations are
   clamped to ±448 with scale 1). 36.4 → 21.3 s/img. Only affects stock ComfyUI, and only
   checkpoints without explicit quant metadata.
2. INT8 ConvRot from BF16 (doc 08): **18.6-18.9 s/img**. The stock comfy-kitchen CUTLASS INT8 path
   is fine on sm_120 (1308 ms vs our Triton kernel 1509 ms per forward), so the GB10 patch is
   **not** installed here.

## What did not

- `torch.compile` in any form (INT8: OOM at 16 GB; NVFP4: dynamo SymInt assertion; FP8 matmul +
  compile: 65-79 s/img).
- NVFP4: faster (15-16 s) but sibling images.
